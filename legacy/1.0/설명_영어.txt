Easily manage cookies and local storage for the current page.

A lightweight management tool that allows you to view, search, edit, and delete all cookie and local storage data on the current tab with a single click of the icon, without opening developer tools.

● View a list of cookies & local storage at a glance
● Real-time search by key or value
● Easily edit and save values with a click
● Delete individual items with a single button

Works instantly without any setup.

You can open it without clicking the icon using the Ctrl + Shift + Y shortcut.
This shortcut can be changed in your browser's settings.
